Meet ED Hardwood Floor

ED Hardwood Floor is a locally operated hardwood flooring company owned by Eliandro Darago. We install, sand, stain, refinish, repair and restore hardwood floors for homeowners and businesses across Connecticut and New York.

Every floor is different, so every project starts with a conversation about what you want and what your floor needs. From there the focus is simple: careful preparation, clean work and a finish you’ll be glad to walk on every day.

Craftsmanship, reliability and customer satisfaction guide how we work. When you call or text, you’re reaching Eliandro directly.
